- **PayPal import: a reversal or refund names the payment it undoes**
  (system_3 #12259).
  - **Before:** a reversal cancelled its payment only when both rows were
    in the same upload. A reversal in a later file, or a refund ze paid
    out, was only counted as skipped, and the Book kept counting the full
    payment.
  - **Now:** the import answer gains `undone`. It has one entry for each
    reversal or outgoing refund whose Reference Txn ID is one of the
    owner's live PayPal payments of money in, in the same currency. Each
    entry gives the payment (`id`, `paid_on`, `sender_name`,
    `gross_cents`), the row's amount, whether it takes back the whole
    payment, and the issued invoice that covers it, if any.
  - A partial refund or reversal is also offered among
    `expense_candidates`, with `refund_of` and `undo_kind`, so the owner
    can count it as money out.
  - Nothing is changed by the import: the counts, the in-file cancelling
    and the stored payment stay as they were. A row that matches nothing
    stays a counted skip.
