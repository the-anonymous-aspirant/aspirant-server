- **Invoices print the issuer's logo** (system_3 #9859, from #9858). A new
  single-row `invoicing_profile_logos` table holds the logo, kept out of the
  profile so no image bytes reach `profile_snapshot` or the profile audit.
  `PUT /invoicing/profile/logo` (multipart field `logo`, behind the
  invoicing owner+Admin gate) accepts a PNG or JPEG up to 5 MB, refuses an
  image over 8000 px on a side or 25 megapixels from its header before
  decoding, trims the fully transparent margin, scales it to 600 px on the
  long side and stores the re-encoded PNG. `GET` serves it and `DELETE`
  removes it; uploads and removals are audited with the sha256, never the
  bytes. `GET /invoicing/profile` gains `has_logo`. Issuing draws the logo
  top right of the header, at most 140×72 pt, above the window-envelope
  recipient line, wraps issuer lines short of it, and records
  `logo_sha256` in the issue audit. An invoice issued without a logo is
  unchanged, and an issued PDF keeps the logo it was printed with.
