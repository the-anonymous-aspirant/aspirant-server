- The Christmas cards, an admin-only picture puzzle (system_3 #12582). Play
  shows one card at a time. The right word, matched without regard to
  capitals or extra spaces, unlocks the next card, and after the last card
  a closing sentence is shown. Play never returns a word, and a card's
  picture is a 404 until the card before it is solved. Control lists the
  words, stores each card's picture (PNG or JPEG, in the card's own row,
  outside the asset store), its word and the sentence, and moves the
  player back to an earlier card. Every route is Admin-only, and a gate
  test walks them all.
