- **A payment is on at most one draft** (system_3 #12263). "Create invoice"
  (`POST /invoicing/invoices`), and a draft edit whose `payment_ids` adds a
  payment, refuse a payment that another draft covers with 422
  `payment_on_draft`, "payment … is already on draft N". A draft may send its
  own payments again. Generate already skipped such a payment. Issue keeps its
  own check unchanged, so of a pair of drafts over one payment made before
  this rule, one still issues and the other is then refused as before.
