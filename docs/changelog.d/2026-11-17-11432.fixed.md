- **Constellations: undo and redo no longer stick behind a step whose other
  player left** (system_3 #11432). `POST .../relationships/undo` and `/redo`
  replay the caller's newest step; when that step would set an edge to a
  player who is no longer in the room, the set was refused and the step
  stayed on top, so every later undo met it again and the older steps were
  out of reach. Another player could cause that by leaving. Such a step is
  now marked done and passed over, and the same request carries on with the
  next one. The response gains `skipped`, the number of steps passed over,
  and, when it is not 0, `notice`, one line to show ("skipped: that player
  left the room"). A step refused for another reason stops as before. The
  app's own screens do not call these two routes today.
