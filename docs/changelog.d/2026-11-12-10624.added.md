- Payments and expenses can be edited (system_3 #10555-A1). A hand-entered row
  may change every field that was typed in; an imported row only its note,
  category and client, since its date, amount and payer are the bank's record.
  A payment on an issued invoice refuses every change, and one on a draft
  keeps the paid-work category. Every edit is audited. A hand-entered expense
  now also stores who it was paid to.
