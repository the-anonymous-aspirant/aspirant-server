- **Invoicing: a notes field on a client** (system_3 #9986, from #9962,
  operator c61516). `GET/POST /api/invoicing/clients` and
  `PUT /api/invoicing/clients/:id` gained a free-text `notes` field
  (trimmed, at most 5000 characters; a PUT without it cleared it). The notes
  are Flower's own: an issued invoice's frozen client snapshot was made to
  leave them out, so they never reach a stored invoice, its PDF or the
  CSV/ZIP exports. The client audit events still record them.
