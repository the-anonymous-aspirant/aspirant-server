- Invoicing: a refused hand-entered payment or expense, an edit of one,
  the issuer profile, a client of the client book and a draft invoice
  now answer a code of their own for each cause, the request field it
  is about (`field`), and the numbers its sentence names (`limit`, and
  `lines` for an address). A draft's line item is refused at its own
  quantity or unit price, with its 1-based position as `item`. A logo
  and an expense receipt answer `missing_file`, `empty_file`,
  `too_large`, `unsupported_type`, and for a logo `unreadable_image`,
  `no_visible_pixels` and `image_too_large` with its `width` and
  `height`. A receipt the server fails to read answers 500, not "larger
  than 5 MB". The codes include `required`, `invalid_date`,
  `date_in_future`, `date_too_early`, `invalid_amount`,
  `amount_too_large`, `too_long`, `hidden_characters`,
  `no_letter_or_digit`, `invalid_email`, `invalid_choice`,
  `invalid_prefix`, `number_too_small`, `no_such_client`,
  `needs_new_client`, `client_and_new_client`, `invalid_category`,
  `below_zero`, `quantity_too_large`, `invalid_line_items` and
  `invalid_vat_rate`.
  Before this, most of them shared `invalid_field`, `bad_request`,
  `invalid_name` or `invalid_address`. A length and a tab or other
  hidden character are now told apart, and a client's two email
  addresses and the profile's name and address are refused one at a
  time (system_3 #12300).
