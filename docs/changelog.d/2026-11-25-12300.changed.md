- Invoicing: a refused hand-entered payment or expense, an edit of one,
  the issuer profile and a client of the client book now answer a code
  of their own for each cause, the request field it is about (`field`),
  and the numbers its sentence names (`limit`, and `lines` for an
  address). The codes include `required`, `invalid_date`,
  `date_in_future`, `date_too_early`, `invalid_amount`,
  `amount_too_large`, `too_long`, `hidden_characters`,
  `no_letter_or_digit`, `invalid_email`, `invalid_choice`,
  `invalid_prefix`, `number_too_small`, `no_such_client`,
  `needs_new_client`, `client_and_new_client` and `invalid_category`.
  Before this, most of them shared `invalid_field`, `bad_request`,
  `invalid_name` or `invalid_address`. A length and a tab or other
  hidden character are now told apart, and a client's two email
  addresses and the profile's name and address are refused one at a
  time (system_3 #12300).
