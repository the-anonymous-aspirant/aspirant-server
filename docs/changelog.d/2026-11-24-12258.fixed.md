- **Invoices: a total never adds two currencies, and the Book counts euros**
  (system_3 #12258).
  - **Before:** cents in any currency were added as euro cents. A US$100.00
    payment added 100.00 to the Book report's euro sums, and a draft over a
    EUR and a USD payment was made, and issued, as one euro total.
  - **The Book report (`/export/book-report.csv`):** it now sums euro rows
    only. A counted row in another currency is left out of every month and
    of Gesamt. When any are left out (in the month asked for, with
    `?month=`), the file's last row says so: "Nicht enthalten: 2 Einträge in
    anderer Währung (USD)". A book in euros only gets the same file as
    before.
  - **Creating a draft, or editing a draft's payments:** payments in more
    than one currency, or in another than the draft's own, are refused with
    422 `mixed_currencies`. Issuing refuses a draft that already holds two.
    Generate makes one draft per payment and is not affected. A draft in one
    currency other than euros is still allowed.
  - **Hand-entered payments and expenses** are in euros: a `currency` other
    than `EUR` is refused with 400. The PayPal import still stores the
    currency its file names.
