- The bookkeeping report (`GET /invoicing/export/book-report.csv`) now counts
  the same rows as the Book, under the owner's current selection (system_3
  #10555-B2): an incoming payment counts when its category is in the
  selection's money in, an expense when its category is in its money out,
  Private never, and removed rows never. Months and expense rows appear only
  for counted entries and categories. This is a stated change: under Work
  only, income no longer includes payments marked Gift, Loan, Refund, Other or
  Uncategorised. The file is named after the selection,
  `book-report-<selection>-<date>-<HHMM>.csv` (`work-only`, a preset's name,
  `-changed` when the ticks differ from it, or `custom`), instead of
  `Buchhaltung-<date>-<HHMM>.csv`.
