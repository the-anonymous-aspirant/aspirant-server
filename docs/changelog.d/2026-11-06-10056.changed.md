- **Invoicing: export filenames carry the export time** (system_3 #10056,
  from #9990 and #9964). The invoice CSV, the client CSV, the ZIP and the
  merged PDF are now sent as `<name>-YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM.<ext>`, the time the
  export was made in Europe/Berlin (e.g. `Rechnungen-2026-11-06-1049.csv`).
  Every export used to carry one fixed name, so an older download could not be
  told from a newer one. The single-invoice PDF keeps `Rechnung-<number>.pdf`.
