- **A client added from an entry gets what that entry knows** (system_3
  #11507). `POST /invoicing/payments/manual` and `PATCH
  /invoicing/payments/:id` take two optional fields beside `new_client_name`:
  `new_client_address` and `new_client_email`, the owner's own typing. They
  are refused without a new client name, so nothing is dropped silently.
  `POST /invoicing/payers` gives a client created from a PayPal payer that
  payer's address as its `email` as well as its `paypal_email`. Only a client
  being created is affected: no existing client or payment row changes, and
  there is no migration.
