- **The client form applies the book's name rule and bounds** (system_3
  #12264). `POST /invoicing/clients`, and a `PUT` that renames, answer 409
  `client_exists` naming the client of the same name (§3.142 Decision 4)
  unless the body sends `confirm_second_client: true`; a kept name is not
  checked, so a stored pair of namesakes stays editable. A name is one line of
  at most 200 characters and an address at most 10 lines of 200, with no tab,
  control or bidirectional character: 400 `invalid_name` or `invalid_address`,
  checked on an edit only when that field changed. A NUL in the notes is a 400
  `invalid_notes`, not a 500. Two clients may still share a PayPal email or an
  IBAN (§3.168 Decision 5).
