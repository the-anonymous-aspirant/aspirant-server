- **Invoices: issuing refuses a total of 0,00, and each refusal carries its
  figures** (system_3 #12274).
  - **Before:** a draft of `0.001 x 0,01 €` was issued with a number and a
    total of 0,00 €, since each line had a quantity and a price above zero.
  - **`POST /invoicing/invoices/:id/issue`** now refuses such a draft with 422
    `total_zero`. No number is used. A total that differs from the payments
    the draft covers stays allowed.
  - **Every refusal the issue answers** keeps its code and message and gains
    an `error.details` object, so the app can word it in its own sentence:
    - `profile_incomplete`: `missing` (`name`, `address`, `steuernummer`);
    - `client_incomplete`: `missing` (`client` or `name`);
    - `line_items_incomplete`: `missing: lines`, or the line's `position`;
    - `service_date_invalid`: `reason` (`missing`, `ends_before_start`,
      `after_today` with its `date`);
    - the payment refusals: `payment_id`. `payment_already_invoiced` also
      gives `invoice_number`, and `payment_not_paid_work` gives `reason`
      (`no_payments` or `not_paid_work`, with `category`);
    - `issue_date_before_previous`: `latest`;
    - `unprintable_characters`: `characters`;
    - `not_a_draft`: `status`;
    - `mixed_currencies`: `currencies`.
  - **The address rule has its own code:** `client_address_required`, with
    `total_cents`, `limit_cents` and `currency`. It was `client_incomplete`.
  - **Saving a draft (`PUT /invoicing/invoices/:id`):** a quantity above
    10,000 is refused with 400 `quantity_too_large` and the line's
    `position`. A price above the bound keeps `invalid_field`.
