- **A draft's service date before 2000 is refused** (system_3 #12312). A
  year typed as "26" gave `0026-11-03`, which a draft saved and which was
  issued with that date printed, for good. Saving a draft now answers 400
  `service_date_invalid` ("service_date_from must be 2000-01-01 or later"),
  as a payment's date already did, and issuing a draft stored earlier with
  such a date answers 422 `service_date_invalid`. A draft may still be dated
  after today; that is refused at the issue, as before. Issued invoices are
  not touched.
