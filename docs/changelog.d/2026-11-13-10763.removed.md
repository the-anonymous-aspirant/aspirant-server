- **The legacy DELETE for payments and expenses** (system_3 #10647-A2,
  design §3.143 Decision 6). `DELETE /invoicing/payments/:id` and
  `DELETE /invoicing/expenses/:id` are gone and answer 404. They erased any
  row not on an issued invoice at once, with no remove in front. Deleting for
  good is now one path: remove the row (`POST …/remove`), then purge it
  (`POST …/purge`), single or bulk. Purge does everything the legacy delete
  did: it unlinks drafts and cancelled invoices, takes an expense's receipt,
  audits the whole row and tombstones it against re-import. It also refuses a
  payment an issued invoice still lists (#10647-A1). No client called either
  route since the restorable Remove shipped (aspirant-client #455), and the
  client's own helpers for them are gone (#10647-A3). `DELETE
  /invoicing/expenses/:id/receipt` is unchanged.
