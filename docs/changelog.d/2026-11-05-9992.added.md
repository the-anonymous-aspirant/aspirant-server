- **Invoicing: an optional phone number on the issuer profile** (system_3
  #9992, from #9967, operator c61727). `GET/PUT /api/invoicing/profile`
  gained an optional `phone` (digits, spaces and `+ - ( ) / .`, at most 40
  characters). When it is set, the invoice PDF prints `Tel.: <phone>` under
  the email in the issuer block; when it is empty, no line was added.
  Invoices issued before this are stored PDFs and kept their original text.
