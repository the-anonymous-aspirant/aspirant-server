- **The import confirms refuse what a hand entry would** (system_3 #12265).
  One row check (`invoicing_row_check.go`) holds the hand entry and the three
  confirms (N26 payments, N26 expenses, PayPal expenses) to the same day,
  amount and characters: a day from 2000-01-01 to today in Berlin (the N26
  payments confirm keeps its one day ahead), an amount above zero and at most
  100 million euros, a payer or payee on one line with no control or invisible
  format character, and a note with no control character but the line break
  and no direction override or isolate. A refused row is named by its day and
  payer or payee ("the row of 2026-10-03 from Mara Esempio: …"), not by its
  place. The N26 statement and transfer-confirmation parses now clean the
  names and notes they offer as the CSV parse does, after a statement row's
  id is made, so every offered row passes and a row already in the book keeps
  its id.
