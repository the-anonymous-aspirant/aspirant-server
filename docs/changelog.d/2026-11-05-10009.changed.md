- **Invoicing: any payment not on an issued invoice can be deleted** (system_3
  #10009, from #9973; operator c61587, scope c61853). `DELETE
  /invoicing/payments/:id` now deletes an imported PayPal or N26 payment as
  well as one entered by hand. It refuses only a payment on an issued
  invoice, with `payment_on_invoice`: cancel that invoice first. The
  `imported_payment` refusal is gone. A payment on a draft is deleted with its
  link: the draft keeps its line items and total, and its `total_mismatch`
  warning now also covers a draft with no payments left. A payment on a
  cancelled invoice is deleted with its link: the invoice keeps its number and
  its stored PDF, but its CSV row no longer lists the deleted payment's
  transaction id. Each delete writes a row to the new
  `invoicing_payment_tombstones` table, so a re-import of the same PayPal or
  N26 file skips it, and the audit event keeps the whole row as `before`. A
  deleted PayPal payment can still be entered by hand. The payments list gains
  `on_issued_invoice`.
