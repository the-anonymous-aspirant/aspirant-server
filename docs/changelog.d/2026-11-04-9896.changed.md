- **Invoicing: a plain "download all" replaces the Jobcenter bundle**
  (system_3 #9896, from #9894). `GET /api/invoicing/export/invoices.zip`
  returns `Rechnungen.zip`, which holds the stored PDF of every issued invoice
  (`Rechnung-<number>.pdf`) and nothing else. Cancelled invoices are left out
  because their stored PDF carries no cancellation mark; the invoice list CSV
  still lists them. `GET /api/invoicing/export/jobcenter-bundle.zip`, its
  `Jobcenter-Nachweise.zip` and its `Zusammenfassung.csv` summary are gone.
  Like every export, the ZIP runs the check first and is refused while it
  reports violations.
- **Invoicing: the check messages name invoice numbers, euros and dates**
  (system_3 #9896). The validation report's `message` fields read, for
  example, "Invoice numbers jump from 2026-002 to 2026-005." or "Invoice
  2026-001 totals €350.00; its payments total €300.00." instead of database
  ids and cents, in the screen's spellings (2026-10-05, €350.00). A message
  about a payment names it by amount, date and category. Rule codes are
  unchanged.
