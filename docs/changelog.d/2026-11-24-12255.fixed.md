- **Issuing an unknown invoice answers 404; a panicking handler answers 500
  and ends its transaction** (system_3 #12255). Issue, edit, delete and
  cancel answer 404 "Invoice not found" for an invoice id they cannot find,
  before any other check. A new middleware, `RecoverPanics`, mounted first,
  turns a panic in any handler into a 500 in the usual error envelope and
  logs the route template and the stack, never the request's headers or
  body. Every `db.Begin()` is followed by `defer tx.RollbackUnlessCommitted()`,
  so a transaction ends on a panic as on every other way out; after a commit
  the deferred call does nothing.
