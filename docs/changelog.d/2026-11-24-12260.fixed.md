- **PayPal import: an amount like `20.0000` is refused instead of stored
  ten thousand times too large** (system_3 #12260).
  - **Before:** with a single separator, a tail of other than one or two
    digits lost its separator. So `20.0000` was stored as 200,000.00 and
    `12.3456` as 123,456.00.
  - **Now:** four or more digits after the last separator are refused
    with the existing "has more than two decimals" message, naming the
    line. Three digits are refused the same way when the file's other
    amounts use that separator for decimals (`12.345` beside `20.00`).
    Otherwise they stay a thousands group, as before (`12,345`).
  - An amount above 100,000,000 euros, the hand entry's ceiling, is
    refused too. A refused file stores nothing.
