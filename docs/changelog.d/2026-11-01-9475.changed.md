- **Changelog entries are one file per PR under `docs/changelog.d/`**
  (system_3 #9455, #9475). Every PR used to insert its entry under
  `## Unreleased` here, so any two open PRs conflicted on the same lines and
  each merge forced a rebase on every sibling. A fragment's filename carries
  its category (`<date>-<task>.<security|added|changed|fixed|removed|deprecated>.md`);
  `scripts/changelog.sh` checks the fragments, renders this file with them
  merged in under the right heading, and `collect` folds them in on a PR of
  its own. `tests/changelog_unit.sh` covers the layouts the aspirant repos use.
  Past entries are unchanged.
