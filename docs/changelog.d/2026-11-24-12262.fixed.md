- **An N26 statement with one long payer or note imports** (system_3
  #12262). The statement and transfer-confirmation parsers now cut an
  offered row's text to the confirm's limits, as the CSV parser does: payer
  200, payment note 500, expense note 1,000, payee 100. The cut is silent
  and on a character boundary, after the row's id is made from the uncut
  text, so a statement imported earlier still reads "already imported". The
  limits are counted in characters everywhere, as the messages say: the
  payment confirm counted bytes, and the CSV parser cut in bytes.
