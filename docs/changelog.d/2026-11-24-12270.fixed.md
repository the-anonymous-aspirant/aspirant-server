- **Invoicing: the profile's text has bounds, and the audit trail comes a
  page at a time** (system_3 #12270).
  - **The profile (`PUT /invoicing/profile`):** the full name and the
    Steuernummer are one line of at most 200 characters, and the address is
    at most 10 lines of at most 200 characters each. Past a bound, the Save
    is refused with 400 `full_name_too_long`, `steuernummer_too_long` or
    `address_too_long`. Before, a name of 1,000,000 characters was saved. A
    field the Save does not change is not checked, so a profile stored over
    a bound can still be saved in its other fields. The other fields keep
    their rules.
  - **The audit trail (`GET /invoicing/audit-events`):** it answers at most
    200 events, newest first, and `next_cursor`, the id to pass as
    `?cursor=` for the next page (null on the last). `?limit=` sets the page
    size, from 1 to 1000. A read of one record's events (`?entity_id=`), as
    the History folds make, still answers all of them unless it passes a
    limit. A bad `limit` or a cursor that is not from an earlier page answers
    400 `invalid_limit` or `invalid_cursor`.
