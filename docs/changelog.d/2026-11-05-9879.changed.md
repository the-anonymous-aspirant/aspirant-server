- **Invoicing: the § 19 note names the Kleinunternehmer exemption**
  (system_3 #9879, from #9878). Since 2026-01-01, § 34a Satz 1 Nr. 5 UStDV
  asks a § 19-exempt invoice to state that the Kleinunternehmer exemption
  applies, not merely that no VAT is charged. The printed note changes from
  "Gemäß § 19 UStG wird keine Umsatzsteuer berechnet." to "Es gilt die
  Steuerbefreiung für Kleinunternehmer gemäß § 19 UStG." (operator confirmed
  on #9656, pending the lawyer's review of the sample invoice). Issued
  invoices are stored PDFs and are unchanged; only invoices issued after
  this ships carry the new line.
