- **Payers from payments into the client book** (system_3 #10548, under
  #10539). `GET /invoicing/payers/candidates` lists the senders of incoming
  payments that have no client yet, one row per PayPal email or per payer
  name (case, accents and punctuation folded), with a tidied client name,
  totals and dates, the book client the payer already is if any, and counts
  of what it leaves out (transfers from PayPal into N26, gifts/loans/
  refunds/other, payments with no name). `POST /invoicing/payers` adds the
  chosen payers and links all their payments in one transaction, carrying
  a PayPal sender's email to the client. A client name that folds to
  nothing (only punctuation) is now refused here and in hand entry.
