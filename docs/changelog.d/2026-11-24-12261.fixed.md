- **Invoices: a draft asks for the money received, and shows what
  issuing will print** (system_3 #12261).
  - **Before:** with VAT on, a draft took each payment as a net price
    and added 19 % on top, so 100,00 received was invoiced as 119,00.
    A draft kept the totals worked out when it was saved, so a change
    of the VAT setting showed only at issue.
  - **Now:** a draft is priced from what was received. Its total is
    its lines, and with VAT on the net and VAT are worked out from
    that total. A draft's figures follow the profile's current VAT
    setting each time it is read; a read writes nothing.
  - The invoice PDF heads gross prices "(brutto)", and the profile
    answer gains `draft_count`. Invoices issued before keep their
    figures.
