Payments and expenses can be removed, restored and deleted for good (system_3 #10555-A2). A removed row leaves the lists, the Book, its CSV, Create invoice and payer matching, and a payment comes off any draft it was on; it is kept, with when it was removed, until it is restored or deleted for good. Deleting for good works only on a removed row and keeps a tombstone of its source and transaction id. Each action takes one row or a list, all or nothing. A payment on an issued invoice cannot be removed.
