- **Invoicing: work expenses, entered by hand, and their categories**
  (system_3 #10414-A, folding in #10424, operator's own ask via #9656
  c63752/c63803). `POST /api/invoicing/expenses/manual` stores a work
  expense (`paid_on`, `amount_cents`, optional `currency`, `category`,
  `reference`, `note`, `client_id`), with the same validation rigor as a
  hand-entered payment (future-dated and pre-2000 refusals,
  control-character and invisible-format-character checks, rune caps).
  `GET /api/invoicing/expenses` lists the owner's expenses, oldest first,
  optionally narrowed by `category`. `DELETE /api/invoicing/expenses/:id`
  removes one — an expense is never invoiced, so this skips every
  issued-invoice check a payment delete runs.

  Categories are not a fixed list: each book is seeded on first use with
  ten starter types (materials and supplies, studio rent and energy,
  insurance and fees, advertising, travel, office and postage, phone and
  internet, advice, training, other), and the owner can add her own on top
  (`GET`/`POST /api/invoicing/expense-categories`). A category's
  `counts_as` names the starter type (Anlage EKS line) its entries add
  into for the eventual report, defaulting to "other"; a user category
  cannot take a starter type's name, and the ten starter types themselves
  are fixed.

  First piece of #10414 (an income-and-expenses book, a bar chart and a
  report export follow in #10418-#10420).
